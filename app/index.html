About This Game

What Does This Teach?

Stella's Rescue Run introduces children to route optimisation - finding the shortest path to visit multiple locations. This is a fundamental problem in computer science and mathematics that helps develop:

  • Logical thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Spatial reasoning and visual planning
  • Understanding of efficiency and optimisation

How Does It Work?

Players plan a route to rescue astronauts whilst managing limited fuel. Through trial and error, they discover that:

  • The order matters - different routes use different amounts of fuel
  • Shorter paths are more efficient than longer ones
  • Visual planning helps identify better solutions

The game progressively increases difficulty by reducing available fuel, encouraging more careful route planning.

How Are Stars Awarded?

Stars reflect how close the player's route is to the optimal solution for that level:

  • ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars: Within 10% of the best possible route
  • ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars: Within 30% of the best possible route
  • ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Better than halfway between worst and best
  • ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Completed but with a poor route
  • ⭐ 1 Star: Barely made it

Each level's star ratings are calibrated to its difficulty, so achieving 5 stars is always possible with the optimal route.

Real-World Applications

Route optimisation is used in:

  • Delivery and logistics (Royal Mail, Amazon)
  • Circuit board design and manufacturing
  • DNA sequencing and bioinformatics
  • Scheduling and resource allocation
